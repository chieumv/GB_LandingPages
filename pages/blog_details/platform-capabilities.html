A trading platform is easier to use when you understand what each capability is meant to support. These four areas are complementary: risk controls help you prepare and follow a plan, while the trading infrastructure, available markets, and charting tools support research and execution.

01. Advanced Risk Management

Risk tools are designed to help make your chosen limits visible before and during trading. Depending on the configured account, these can include daily loss boundaries, risk-per-trade planning, session rules, and equity protection. They do not remove market risk or guarantee a result, but they can help you apply a clearer process.

  • Read the Guard guide for an introduction to account-level risk rules.
  • Read the Risk Amount guide to understand estimated order sizing from Balance and Stop Loss distance.
  • Read the Auto-Lock SL/TP guide to understand its review and locking timeline.

02. Powered by MetaTrader 5

MetaTrader 5 is the trading infrastructure used to connect accounts, quotes, and orders. It provides the underlying environment for monitoring positions and managing trades. Execution can still be affected by market conditions, liquidity, connection quality, and the terms that apply to an account.

03. Global Market Access

Eligible GreatBless accounts can provide access to CFDs across markets such as Forex, Metals, Energies, Indices, and Crypto. The instruments you can trade, their conditions, and whether a product is available depend on your account, jurisdiction, and applicable restrictions. Review the product details before opening a position.

04. TradingView Integration

TradingView charting is integrated into the Web Trader to help you examine price action, compare timeframes, and use charting tools as part of your analysis. Charting can support research; it is not a prediction of future prices and should be used alongside your own risk plan.

Explore the TradingView integration in detail.

Where to begin

If you are new to the platform, begin with a demo account and focus on one capability at a time: set out the risk rule you want to follow, practise reading a chart, then review how an order behaves. Trading involves risk and losses can occur.
